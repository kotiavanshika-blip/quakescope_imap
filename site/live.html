Latency

p50 / p95 / p99

p50: half of the requests are faster. p95: 95 out of 100 are faster. p99: 99 out of 100 are faster. Slow requests show up in p95 and p99 first.

Error rate

% of requests

The share of requests that fail with a server error (HTTP status 500 to 599).

Break it on purpose

Each button sends test traffic to our own API for 30 seconds so you can watch the graphs react. Expect a delay of 5 to 15 seconds, because Prometheus collects new numbers every 5 seconds.

Cause errors sends many requests to /test/error, which fails 9 times out of 10. Watch the error rate jump.
Cause slow requests sends requests to /test/slow, which waits 2 seconds. Watch p95 and p99 rise. The error rate stays flat, because slow requests still succeed.
